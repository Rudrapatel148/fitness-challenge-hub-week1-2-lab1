Welcome to the FitSquad Hub!

FitSquad Hub is a community fitness challenge platform designed to make working out more exciting, social, and rewarding. Members can participate in fitness quests, track their workout duration, and earn XP reward points as they progress.

Whether you're just beginning your fitness journey or looking for a tougher challenge, FitSquad Hub offers difficulty levels ranging from Beginner to Advanced. Join the community, complete challenges, earn XP, and keep pushing toward your fitness goals!
